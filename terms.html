PawVago is a planning tool for non-commercial travel with pets and routine pet-care recordkeeping. The current beta is provided without a paid subscription.

1. Preliminary guidance, not official approval

Airline rules, entry requirements, timelines and suggested checklists may be incomplete or outdated. PawVago does not guarantee that an animal will be accepted by a carrier or admitted into a country. Always check the operating airline, official national authorities and a qualified veterinarian before purchasing tickets or crossing a border.

2. No booking, emergency response or veterinary advice

The current service does not book tickets, reserve cabin pet space, provide verified live fares, diagnose illness, prescribe medications or provide emergency veterinary services. In emergencies, contact a qualified veterinarian or local emergency services directly.

3. Your account and records

You are responsible for maintaining the accuracy of the animal's profile, vaccine dates, document dates and travel inputs. In-app reminders do not currently send alerts in the background, so do not rely on PawVago as your only source of time-critical notifications. Keep your login credentials private.

4. Nearby service listings

Nearby results may be incomplete, unverified or unavailable. External map services and third-party providers operate independently. Confirm location, opening hours, service suitability, prices and availability with the provider directly.
