# PawVago Multimodal Travel Update

Proposed product expansion for investor review before production merge.

## Transport modes
- Flight — airline cabin/cargo rules, carrier limits, breed restrictions and route requirements.
- Train — operator pet policies, tickets, carrier/muzzle rules and route requirements.
- Ferry / Ship — pet cabins, kennels, deck access and operator requirements.
- Car — cross-border requirements, documents, pet-friendly stops and road-trip planning.
- Multimodal — combine air, rail, sea and road segments in one trip.

## Product architecture
Trip → Segments → Transport Mode → Operator → Pet Rules → Requirements

Example:
- Nantes → Paris | Train | SNCF
- Paris → Vienna | Flight | Airline
- Vienna → Budapest | Train | ÖBB

## Proposed website positioning
**Travel anywhere. Together.**

Plan compliant journeys with your pet by air, rail, road and sea — all in one place.

## Proposed website changes
- Replace airline-only messaging with multimodal pet mobility messaging.
- Add Air, Rail, Road, Sea and Multimodal travel choices.
- Update trip planner language to support transport operators rather than airlines only.
- Update feature cards and product preview language.
- Preserve the existing design system and investor-ready presentation.
