Privacy Policy

Effective: 29 October 2026 · Beta service

PawVago is a pet-travel planning and everyday pet-care companion. This notice explains how personal data is handled on pawvago.com. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Controller and contact

For the current beta, the contact for personal-data requests is Nasrin VATANKHAH, PawVago project founder, based in France. You may contact contact@pawvago.com to request access, correction or deletion, or to ask privacy questions.

2. Information we process

When you register, we process your email and account authentication data. If you choose to add a pet, trip or veterinarian, we store the profile, relevant dates, destinations, reminders, and clinic contact details that you enter. Personal veterinary notes and document reference dates may be sensitive in context: please avoid including unnecessary information about yourself or other people. We do not currently offer uploading private veterinary document images in Everyday Mode.

When you use the public travel tools, we process the questions and trip details you submit to generate preliminary responses. These public tools do not require an account; do not enter passwords, document scans or highly sensitive personal information. Server hosting providers may process standard security and technical logs.

3. Purpose and legal basis

We use account and pet data to deliver saved profiles, records, care reminders and trip planning requested by you (contractual service). We use limited service diagnostics and security information to protect and operate PawVago (legitimate interests). Browser geolocation is only requested after you choose Use my location, subject to your browser's permission. We do not store your GPS coordinates in the PawVago database for nearby provider searches.

4. Nearby services and external links

Nearby search attempts to match a temporary location against available PawVago directory listings. When you choose to open Google Maps, the search location and your use of Google Maps are handled separately by Google under its own terms and privacy practices. PawVago does not guarantee provider opening hours, pricing or availability.

5. Where data is held

PawVago uses Supabase to provide account authentication and structured user records and Vercel to host the website and API functions. Our Supabase project currently uses an EU West region. Other providers, security infrastructure and external links may process information outside your country; relevant contractual safeguards should be reviewed as the service expands internationally.

6. Sharing

Your stored pet profiles, saved document dates, reminders and preferred veterinarian records are intended to be visible only to your authenticated account, protected by database row-level security. The public ChatGPT tools are intended to use only the reviewed airline-policy directory, not your private care records. We do not currently provide public pet-owner location sharing or automatically send your personal records to nearby providers.

7. Storage and retention

Saved account content remains while your account is active unless you delete records or ask us to delete your account, subject to legitimate technical or legal retention obligations. Where deletion cannot be performed self-service in this beta, email contact@pawvago.com. We aim to respond to data-rights requests within applicable legal deadlines. Backups and security logs may have separate limited retention.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or transfer your personal information, or object to processing. In France you can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

9. Notifications and purchases

Saved care reminders currently appear inside your account when you revisit the site. PawVago does not yet send automatic push, SMS or email care alerts. Paid subscriptions are not enabled in this beta. Any later AI API, payment, push-notification, document-upload or authenticated ChatGPT connection may require an updated notice before activation.

10. Children, changes and contact

The service is not designed for children to register independently. We will update this notice when features or processors materially change. For questions, write to contact@pawvago.com.
